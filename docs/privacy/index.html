Privacy, by the grill.

Privacy policy · Last updated November 1, 2026

Grill Master is a grilling timer and cook log. This policy explains data handled by the app, this support website, and the support channels linked here.

Your cook records stay local. Grill Master does not require an account or operate a server for your cooking history. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data on your device

The app stores cook information locally, including saved cook names, cooking settings, ratings you choose to record, and timer or queue state used to continue a cooking session. It uses device preferences or the app’s local web storage. These records are not uploaded to a Grill Master account or server.

Grill Master does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone to run its cooking features. A name you type for a saved cook is part of that local record.

Local records remain until removed through available app controls or cleared with the app’s data. Deleting the app may remove its local records; offloading an app is different and may retain data. Device backups and restores may preserve or restore app data according to your Apple and device settings. Grill Master does not provide its own cloud sync or backup service.

In a browser version, clearing this site’s storage removes locally stored records for that browser profile. Browser and native app storage are separate.

Notifications and permissions

Grill Master can request permission to show local cooking reminders. When enabled, the device schedules and displays these notifications through iOS. No push-notification server is used for those reminders. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings.

The app can also use device haptics and play timer sounds. Notification delivery and sound depend on device settings and operating-system behavior.

Apple, GitHub, and external links

App Store and TestFlight

Apple handles app purchases, downloads, and any TestFlight participation. Apple may process purchase, diagnostic, or testing information under its own policies and your settings. Grill Master does not receive your payment-card details. Information you deliberately share through TestFlight feedback is handled through Apple’s testing service.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The pages do not include analytics scripts, tracking pixels, embedded third-party content, external fonts, or forms. GitHub may log technical request information, including IP addresses, when serving the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of that information.

Support requests

Support links open GitHub Issues. If you choose to post an issue, GitHub processes your account and submitted content. Public issues can be read by others. Share only details needed to explain the problem; do not post private contact details, purchase receipts, or other sensitive information. The project maintainers can read and respond to the information you submit.

Links to other websites open services with their own privacy policies.

Questions and updates

For a question about this policy, use the Grill Master issue tracker. Keep public questions free of personal information.

This page will be updated when the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
