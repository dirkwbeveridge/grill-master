Start with a cook plan

  1. Choose gas or charcoal, then select your protein and cut.
  2. Set thickness where available, heat, and doneness. Review any weather or altitude adjustments.
  3. Check the estimated sear, finish, and rest stages, then start your cook.
  4. Follow the stage prompts and check the food’s internal temperature with a thermometer.
  5. Save a named cook in the Log when you want to keep its settings for another day.

Common questions

Are the times a guarantee that food is done?

No. Starting temperature, grill output, thickness, and conditions vary. The timer estimates cooking stages; it does not measure your food. Use a food thermometer and the safe minimum internal temperature for the food you are preparing. Appearance, elapsed time, and a preferred doneness label alone do not establish food safety.

Why didn’t I hear a timer alert?

Check Grill Master’s notification permissions in iOS Settings, then check sound volume and Focus settings. Silent mode and operating-system behavior can affect alerts. Keep an eye on the cook and use the visible timer as well. If alerts are unreliable, report the device, iOS version, app version, and whether the phone was locked or the app was in the background.

How do I cook more than one item?

Use the Queue to add items and follow each item’s stage. Check the item’s name before pausing, continuing, or ending it. Each item still needs its own thermometer check.

Where are my saved cooks?

Open the Log. Cook records are stored on the device or browser profile where you saved them. They do not sync through a Grill Master account. Clearing app or browser storage, changing devices, or reinstalling may affect those records. Device backups may behave differently; see the privacy policy.

Do I need an internet connection?

The installed app bundles its cooking interface and cooking data. Opening support, privacy, GitHub, or other external pages requires a connection. App installation and updates use Apple’s services.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem service and follow its instructions. Eligibility and decisions are handled by Apple.

Report a problem

Search the existing issues first. For a new report, include:

  • Your device model, iOS version, and Grill Master version/build.
  • The steps that led to the problem and what you expected.
  • The protein, cut, thickness, heat, and stage involved, if relevant.
  • Whether the app was foregrounded, backgrounded, or the device was locked.
  • A screenshot with personal information removed, if it helps.

For privacy questions, read the privacy policy. For purchase and billing problems, use Apple’s support services.
