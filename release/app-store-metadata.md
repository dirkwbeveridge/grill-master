## Description

A little timing. A better cook.

Grill Master: Backyard Pro is a grilling timer and a pitmaster’s notebook. Build an estimated cook plan, follow your cooking stages, and save the settings you want to use again.

PLAN YOUR COOK
Choose your protein and cut, set thickness where available, and select heat and doneness. Use gas or charcoal settings, with optional cold-weather and altitude adjustments.

FOLLOW THE STAGES
See the plan for searing each side, finishing, and resting. Pause or end a cook as needed, and follow the stage prompts while you grill.

KEEP THE GRILL ORGANIZED
Use the Queue for multiple items. Save a named cook in the Log to keep a record of its settings and the result you choose to record.

CHECK YOUR TEMPERATURES
Keep cooking-temperature guidance close by. Estimated times are a starting point: Grill Master does not measure food temperature. Always use a food thermometer and follow safe minimum internal temperatures for the food you are cooking.

YOUR NOTEBOOK STAYS WITH YOU
No account is required. Cook records are stored locally on your device. The installed cooking interface and cooking data are bundled with the app; support and other external links require a connection. Device backup and restore behavior depends on your settings.
